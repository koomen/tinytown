# Blueprint notes — 248273911 — Avon Post Office, 80 Prospect St

## Identification
United States Post Office, Avon NY 14414. The `+u` photo shows the brick
lettering "UNITED STATES POST OFFICE AVON, NEW YORK / 14414" head-on.
Confidence: high.

## Frame
OBB 19.0 m (u) x 15.5 m (v); +u bears 285° (WNW) toward Prospect St, +v bears
15° (NNE). Plain rectangle, flat lot. Road face `+u` is the 15.5 m gable end;
the ridge runs along u (the long axis), so the long flanks are `+v` (toward the
small brick "Frontier" house 248274488) and `-v` (toward Kurtz-Hodge 248273912).

## Photos used
- `front_248273911_+u.png` — head-on, the main source. Clear.
- `front_248273911_+v.png`, `front_248273911_+v_2.png` — oblique from the NNE;
  used for the long-side window rhythm, roof form and the rear chimney.
- `front_248273911_-u.png` — 178° off head-on, i.e. it is the front again; no
  rear view exists. Rear modelled plain.
- `front_248273911_-v.png` — a street tree fills the frame; `-v_2.png` is the
  Kurtz-Hodge building (the neighbour), not this one. -v face modelled plain,
  mirroring +v without shutters.

## Reading face by face
- `+u` (road, 15.5 m, left end = NNE): one storey of red-orange brick to a
  white horizontal band at the eave (~3.5 m), then a low front gable clad in
  white vertical siding with white raked fascia; ridge ~6.6 m. Below the band,
  pale block lettering on the brick over the left ~0.1–0.6 of the face. Three
  6/6 sash windows with navy shutters at ~0.16, 0.35, 0.54, sills ~0.9 m.
  Right of centre (~0.75) a white gabled entry box with a glass door and a
  small lamp; blank brick right of it. Tall flagpole ~5 m out in front at ~0.42.
  Low clipped hedge across the window bays; two big round bushes flanking the
  entry. Roof dark grey asphalt.
- `+v` (19 m flank): brick, a regular row of ~5–6 shuttered windows along the
  length, gable roof continuous to the rear, brick chimney near the rear end.
- `-v`, `-u`: not photographed. Plain: a window row on -v, two windows and a
  service door on the rear.

## What was modelled / exaggerated
- Single gable volume, eaves 3.8 m, `pitch 0.19` (rise over full span; ~2.9 m
  ridge rise), white `gableColor` siding, white belt course at the eave line.
- Lettering as a `carved` sign with `opts.bg` = wall colour so it reads as
  paint on brick without a transparent plane; centred at 0.34 over the
  windows, 8.2 x 0.6 m (letters ~0.28 m, about real size but legible).
- Three windows 1.3 x 1.8 m with navy shutters (bigger than real, same rhythm).
- Gabled entry porch 2.4 m wide, white, dark shingle roof, lamp, one step.
- `flag` detail: 9 m pole ~5 m out in front, 1.5 m flag — the signature.
- Hedge as a row of five bushes plus two larger ones flanking the entry.
- Chimney near the rear on the +v slope. Five shuttered windows on +v.

## Approximations / schema gaps
- The "14414" second line under the lettering was dropped (too small).
- The real siding is vertical board with battens; rendered as a flat colour.
- Porch roof tiles look slightly jagged at 45 m; fine up close.
- Rear (`-u`) and `-v` are guesses (no imagery).
- From the `+v` camera at 45 m the generic neighbour house 248274488 hides
  most of the flank; the visible part matches.

## Confidence
High on identity, massing, roof form, colours, window rhythm, entry and
flagpole. Medium on exact eave/ridge heights (+-0.4 m). Low on the rear and
-v elevations.
