# Blueprint notes — OSM 248251396 (overhead label 1396)

## Identification
49 Park Place, Avon NY — offices of **The Tilly Agency (insurance) / Burke Associates**, in a
converted white 2-storey clapboard house on the south side of the Memorial Park circle, directly
east of the Avon Fire Department office (4709) and west of the 3-storey mansard house (1271).
Street View (Aug 2025) close-up shows the door numbered "49" with a "Home" sign, and the hanging
green board "The Tilly Agency Insurance / BURKE" between the porch posts. Confidence high on identity.

Sources: `front_248251396_+u.png` (from the circle, tree partly in the way), `front_248251396_+u_2.png`
(18 m, door "49"), `front_248251396_-v.png` and `_-v_2.png` (head-on-ish from the NW corner of the
circle — clearest view of the roof composition and the west gable end), plus the older obliques
`sv_248251396_a/b.png`.

## Frame
OBB 12.8 × 12.9 m; +u = north (16°, fronts Park Place), +v = east (106°). Face +u fractions run
from the EAST end (left as seen from the road).

## Reading, face by face
**North front (+u):** the house is really two roof volumes side by side:
- East half: a 2-storey block with a **gable facing the street** (ridge N–S). Upstairs 2 windows with
  black shutters. Ground floor (behind the porch): one window + the dark office storm door.
- West half: a 2-storey block with **ridge E–W**, its **gable end facing west** (fire station side),
  carrying one shuttered window and a small round attic vent. Upstairs on the north face 2 shuttered
  windows towards the east end, blank to the west. Ground floor: big window under the hanging sign,
  then the light "49 / Home" door, then a window.
- **Wraparound porch** across the whole front on ~7 slim white square posts with a low dark hip roof;
  it returns around the east corner and a short way along the west side. Sign board hangs between
  posts right of centre; flower bed and clipped shrubs in front.
**West (-v):** gable end with window + round vent; porch return with a post.
**East (+v):** side of the east gable block; porch return; 2-3 windows upstairs (from oblique a).
**South:** not visible from any street; filled with a low hip "rear-block".

## What was modelled (caricature)
- `east-gable-block` (gable ridge u), `west-wing` (gable ridge v, round attic window on -v), `rear-block`
  (hip) — the two-gable roofline plus the round vent is the roof signature.
- Standing-seam grey metal roofs `#6d7175`, white clapboard `#e9e9e4`, near-black shutters `#25282c`.
- Porch: the schema only has a gabled entry porch, so the open wraparound porch is approximated as three
  low flat-roofed volumes (front + two returns) in a shaded mid-grey `#8f8c86` with white **pilasters**
  as the posts, a white cornice as the fascia, and the two doors, ground-floor windows and the green
  "The Tilly Agency" board placed on the porch face. Reads as a shadowed porch with white posts.
- `details`: green lawnsign "The Tilly Agency" in front of the west door (as requested), 4 bushes.

## Not expressible / approximated
- Open porch with posts and a hip porch roof (see above; flat roof + pilasters).
- The porch floor is raised ~0.6 m in reality; doors sit at grade with 1 step.
- Hanging sign has two lines ("Insurance", "Burke Associates") — one line only.
- Rear of the house unknown.

## Confidence
High on the front/west composition, roof forms, colours and porch; medium on the east face and the
exact number/spacing of porch posts; low on the rear.
