## Identification
Two-storey dark cedar-shingle house, long side to the road, at 47 North Avenue, between 37 (south) and the grey-stucco 55 North Avenue (north). Brown asphalt gable roof with the ridge parallel to the road, ribbon of first-floor windows under white trim, recessed entry porch under the roof at the south end, a one-storey screened porch with a red door on the south gable end, a low tan one-storey wing at the north end, brick chimney near the north end of the ridge. - Ribbon window as three touching tall windows, two 2nd-floor windows and the little square window over the entry.
- Open porch (hip roof, 2 posts, 1 step) for the recessed entry; door lifted onto its floor.
- Screened porch as a small volume with dark-glass panels and the red door; tan wing with hip roof.

- Saltbox (asymmetric gable) not expressible; modelled as a symmetric gable.
- Skylights and the split-rail fence not modelled.
- Footprint coverage 67%: the OSM outline is deeper/wider than the photographed massing; rear may include an unseen addition.
