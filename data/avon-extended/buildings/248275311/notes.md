# Kraft Heinz wall sign

Added 2026-09-14 from the user's close-up factory photograph. The cached
August 2025 Street View `front_248275311_+u.png` in
`data/avon-expanded--92bb1889fe--expansion-0041/research/` confirms the white
sign near the eastern (image-left) end of the Spring Street-facing wall.

The sign sits on `northeast_loading.faces.+u`, at face fraction 0.13,
6.8 m above the building base. Its 4.8 × 2.8 m white board and 0.32 m wall
offset are photo-based miniature estimates, not surveyed dimensions. It
clears the facade pilasters and remains separate from the loading doors.

`textures/kraft-heinz-sign.svg` uses the navy/red wordmark paths from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KraftHeinz.svg),
whose source is the company's fact sheet. The surrounding white panel and
wordmark spacing follow the user's photo. Paths keep the lettering independent
of installed fonts. The same sign is stored in `overrides.json` and `site.json`.
