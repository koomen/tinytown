## What was modelled / exaggerated
- Hip roof (ridge along v) with 0.55 m overhang, plus a rear cross-gable volume (ridge u) with arched attic windows on both gable ends.
- Front gable dormer as `roof.dormers` on the hip's `-v` slope: one centred gable-style dormer, dark green shingle colour with white trim and the roof colour on its cap, sill 0.6 m above the eave, a single wide arched window (2.3 m) — the signature feature, drawn as one big arch instead of the real arch-plus-sidelights.
- Full-width open porch with railing and centred steps; dark door; wide flanking ground-floor windows; the small arched 2nd-floor window kept.
- Red brick chimney right of the dormer.
- Two bushes at the foot of the porch.

## Approximations / schema gaps
- The dormer primitive is a box (window width + 0.5 m) with one window, so the wide real dormer with an arch flanked by two small lights becomes one oversized arch; its peak sits just under the main ridge.
- Seen from the street, the rear cross-gable's slopes show above the hip's side slopes with the contour-shaded shingle bands — geometrically right, slightly busy.
- Rear bulkhead/shed, the leaded transom on the front window and the stone/grey foundation drop at the rear are omitted or reduced to a 0.5 m plinth.
- Front window pair on the ground floor made slightly wider than real to read behind the porch.

## Confidence
High on identification, roof form and street face; medium on the +u bay positions (oblique photo); low on the rear (+v) and -u faces (no coverage, inferred).
