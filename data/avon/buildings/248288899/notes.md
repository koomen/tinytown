# Blueprint notes — OSM 248288899 (house, Temple St west side, north end)

## Identification
Tan pebble-dash stucco upright-and-wing house immediately SSW of 35 Temple St (248288900), with a detached garage (248288898) behind it to the SSW. No address in OSM. Confidence high: the `-u` photo is head-on (15° off) and the neighbour bearings match — white clapboard 35 Temple with its screened porch and flag to the right (NNE), the garage with a carport to the left (SSW).

## Frame
OBB 13.1 m (u, WNW–ESE) × 10.2 m (v, NNE–SSW). Road face `-u` (Temple St, looks ESE); left end (0) = `-v` = SSW. Rectangle footprint; both volumes span the full depth. Flat lot (0.4 m).

## Reading face by face
- `-u` (road): left ~55 % is a two-storey upright with its gable to the street: two tall narrow navy-trimmed double-hung windows over two, a small louvred vent in the peak, shallow-pitched gable. Right ~45 % is a low one-storey wing behind a full-width open porch: flat/near-flat roof on slate-blue posts, white railing on the right half, white six-light door at the left of the porch (against the upright), one window to the right of it, two concrete steps in front of the door. Brown shingle roofs; brick chimney rising from the wing roof behind the porch.
- `+v` (toward 35 Temple): wing side, low eaves, hip-looking roof, chimney; windows mostly hidden by shrubs.
- `-v` (toward the garage): plain two-storey upright side, a few rectangular windows.
- `+u` (rear): not photographed; kept plain with a back door on the upright.

## What was modelled / exaggerated
- Two volumes: `upright` (v -5.1..0.4, 5.8 m eaves, gable ridge along u, ridge +2.0 m) and `wing` (v 0.2..5.1, 3.6 m eaves, hip roof +1.2 m).
- Signature features made a touch bigger: tall narrow navy-trimmed windows (0.95 × 1.6 m) in the 2-over-2 rhythm, the peak vent, the flat-roofed porch with slate-blue posts and white railing, the white door with navy surround, the brick chimney on the wing.
- Wing `+v` face given two windows, `+u` one; upright `-v` three-over-two.
- Two bushes in the front lawn toward 35 Temple.

## Approximations / schema gaps
- Wing roof form chosen as a hip: the street photo shows a slope facing the street and the oblique `+v` photos show a slope facing NNE; a hip satisfies both without a definitive view.
- The wing is probably shallower than the full 13.1 m depth in reality (the rear likely steps down), but the footprint is a plain rectangle so both volumes span it.
- Rear (+u) and the lower part of the `-v` side are inferred.
- Chimney colour is the schema default (brick), which matches.
- Porch posts are slimmer than the real chunky square posts; no schema knob for post width.
- Render checks: `-u` compare matches well. `+v` compare could not be judged — the camera lands inside 35 Temple's roof at any distance because the gap is only 6.7 m. `-v` compare is fine (the generic garage occludes the left edge).

## Confidence
High on the road face (massing, bay rhythm, porch, colours). Medium on the wing roof form and depth. Low on the rear.
