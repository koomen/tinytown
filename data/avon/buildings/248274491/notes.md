## Approximations / schema gaps
- The right-hand front section's roof is really the main slope continuing past a kink; modelled as a separate lower hip-roofed wing.
- The real awning's gable is an open canopy on posts; the schema's gabled entry box is a closed porch, so it reads as a small vestibule.
- Window counts on the sides are reduced (fewer, larger); rear face guessed.
- Side-face `--compare` renders: `+v` works (neighbour roof in the foreground but the upper wall is visible); `-v` could not be checked — the camera at 45 m and 25 m both end up inside the generic block of 70 W Main (248274492, 6.4 m away), so the render is a wall of noise.
