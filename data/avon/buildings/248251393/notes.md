# Blueprint notes — OSM 248251393 — 119 Genesee Street

## Identification
119 Genesee St, a 2½-storey olive-green Queen Anne house with a full-width front porch, immediately NNE of the white 121 Genesee (248251392). Street number "119" is legible on the porch fascia in the head-on photo. Confidence: high.

## Frame
OBB 17.4 m (u) × 11.9 m (v); +u bears 106° ESE (road face, 11.9 m wide), +v bears 196° SSW (toward 121 Genesee). Left end of the road face = +v = SSW. Flat lot. The OBB includes the ~2.4 m deep porch, so the house body runs u ≈ -8.7…6.3 and the porch 6.3…8.7.

## Reading face by face
- **+u (street)**: full-width porch on a raised floor (~0.6 m), white square posts (4–5), white railing with balusters, red-painted steps right of centre (~0.6). Left bay: wide triple window under the porch; centre-right: white storm door (~0.6). Right end of the porch is walled in siding with a big round-arched opening leading to the dark red front door, with its own steps. Upstairs: a narrow window pair over the left bay and a taller window with transom at ~0.66. Above: steep front gable over the left half with a round-arched attic window; the right half is the hip roof.
- **-v (NNE)**: two windows per floor near the front, a side door with steps at mid-length on a rock-faced stone foundation (~0.6 m), cross gable with a small attic window, lower hipped rear ell with fewer windows.
- **+v (SSW)**: cross gable with arched attic window near the front, paired windows both floors, a/c unit; rear ell plain.
- **-u (rear)**: not photographed; kept plain with a back door.
- Colours: olive-khaki clapboard, cream trim, dark grey asphalt shingles, grey stone foundation, dark red front door.

## What was modelled / exaggerated
- Main hip block (eaves 6.6 m) + a steep front-gable volume over the left half of the front (arched attic window enlarged) + a cross volume giving gables on both long sides (arched attic windows) + a lower hipped rear ell.
- Open porch full width with 5 posts, railing, 4 red-ish steps at 0.6; the arched siding opening is a small flat-roofed vestibule volume under the porch roof carrying a wide dark-red arched door with its own 3 steps.
- Stone plinth 0.6 m on all house volumes; three bushes in front (the two round shrubs flanking the steps and the big one at the arch).
- Fewer, larger windows than reality; same rhythm.

## Approximations / schema gaps
- The two cross gables are a single ridge-v volume so they sit at the same u; in reality the +v gable is slightly further forward than the -v one.
- The arched opening is really an open arch in the siding (you look through it to the door); modelled as a dark arched door in a cream surround.
- Open-porch hip roof renders very light on its flat top from above (renderer lighting), though its colour is set dark.
- +v render is blocked by 121 Genesee's generic mass at 35 m; the +v face was checked only in the photo.

## Confidence
Massing/roof/porch: high. Window positions: medium-high. Rear face: low (unphotographed).
