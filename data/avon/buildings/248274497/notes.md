## Approximations / schema gaps
- The garage sticks 1.8 m past the OBB on the SE side (`v` to -6.0) — a lint warning, deliberate: in the photos the garage/driveway lie outside the OSM rectangle; without it the door would be hidden behind the house.
- The NW rear lean-to and back stoop are omitted (they would fall outside the OBB too).
- A "LUNCH" feather flag on the front lawn was tried as a `lawnsign` but rendered as a dark edge-on slab from both rotations; dropped.
- `bush` `height` does not seem to make a tall column; the arborvitae reads as a small shrub.
- Windows are fewer and larger than reality; AC units, storm doors and the metal railing's pattern ignored.
- Two overlapping gable volumes with ridges 1 m apart give a small roof seam on the SE slope; acceptable at diorama scale.

## Confidence
High on massing, colours, porch and the duplex front; medium on the exact depth of the SE step-out and the garage position (read from oblique photos and a low-res tile); low on the rear (-u) face.
