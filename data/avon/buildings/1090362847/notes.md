# 1090362847 — 15 West Main Street (OSM address; street number on the door is 11)

## Identification
Tiny two-storey red-brick Italianate infill wedged between the Wadsworth
Building (corner block, 248274499) and the cream-corniced 13-17 W Main block
(248274500). Flat roof with a plain brick parapet and a thin cream coping; no
business sign. Confidence high — clearly seen head-on in
`front_1090362847_-u.png` (right half) and in the left part of
`front_248274500_-v.png`; also sv_1090362847_a/b.
