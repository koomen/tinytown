# Lenna Hall revision — 2026-09-14

The aerial footprint and located Google Street View captures show an eight-sided main hall with clipped corners, broad flared eaves over a continuous veranda, and a lower angled rear annex. Replaced the previous overlapping rectangular hall/shoulder roofs. The main outline follows the mapped outer roof edge; walls sit 2.3 m inside it. Porch details and heights are approximations from the photographs; stairs sample the rendered terrain.

References: `aerial_820059627_footprint.png`, `front_820059627_+u.png` (ENE front, Google Street View July 2012), `front_820059627_-u.png` (rear); user supplied front image. Front portals, pale masonry piers, dark timber rails, clerestory and the full ELIZABETH S. LENNA HALL name follow the visible front.

Official exterior photograph: https://www.chq.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/lenna_hall.jpg
